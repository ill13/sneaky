SNEAK RUN v0.1.0

3 KEYS → DOORS → FILE → EXIT  ·  never get caught twice.

  1. Find the three colored keys (blue / gold / red). They're hidden inside furniture - desks, cabinets, copiers, the safe. Walk up to a piece and hold ACT to search it (a green ring fills while you work). You don't know where the keys are yet - the notes left in the furniture do. Search the rooms up top first and read what you find: each note names a room, and that room lights up on the minimap. Then go get the key. Each key opens its matching door on the sealed bottom row - the red door guards the FILE, the blue door guards the EXIT, the gold one opens a side trip. Steal the file, reach the exit.
  2. Guards see in cones you can't see. The minimap (top right) lights up each room as you explore it - rooms you haven't walked stay dark, and you can't see guards or containers in them. Bank an UPGRADE and it reveals their cones. A ? warns a guard is turning your way; a ! means it's spotted you (red = looking, yellow = searching). Break line of sight for 3s and they lose you.
  3. One hit = ALARM: the compound goes hot (faster, sharper, they stop giving up). Stay hidden 10s and it cools. A second hit = CAUGHT.
  4. One ACT button does it all by context: behind a guard you knock it out for 10s; next to a downed one you grab it and carry it to a bin to hide it; pressed toward a wall you distract the guards in the room - the noise is the wall you're facing, and they'll walk over to investigate (they can't hear through doors, and a guard already chasing you won't break off); held against a piece of furniture you search it. Guard actions always beat a distraction, and carrying a body leaves your hands full - you can only hide it or drop it.
  5. Some guards are stuck at a post (watch for the pedestal) - only their head turns, in 90-degree steps. Cross when the gaze swings away, or get behind it and knock it out. And a body left in the open gets found and shaken awake by a passing guard, so get it to a bin fast.
  6. The map is a loop with a detour: the file is deep, the exit is a full sweep back. Dead-end rooms off the path hold UPGRADES (V speed, H hush, W weight) - tucked in a piece of furniture to search. Exploring costs time, and it pays out for the rest of the run.
